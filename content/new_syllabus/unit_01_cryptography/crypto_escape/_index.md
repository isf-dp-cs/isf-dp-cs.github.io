# Welcome to the Crypto Escape Room!

To get out of each "room" (webpage) of this escape room, you will need to solve the puzzle to find the password. Once you have that password, you can use it to move on to the next page by appending it to the URL. For example, if you think the password to this room is "password", you should add `/password` to the end of the URL after `crypto_escape`. 

You will need to use the labs to help you escape. 

---

There's a hacker in ISF attempting to release the personal information of students. It's your responsiblity to save the school! 

The head of ISF's IT brings you to a supercomputer in the middle of the Head of School's conference room on A11. There are a series of numbers printed on the console.
